What are the early warning signs of a stroke? What should I do immediately?
Stroke warning signs follow the FAST acronym:

Face drooping · Arm weakness · Speech difficulty · Time to call emergency services

Additional signs include sudden severe headache, vision changes in one or both eyes, and loss of balance or coordination.

Privacy & Trust

Your data is yours, always

Medical information is deeply personal. We've built MIRA from the ground up with privacy as a core principle.

✓
Messages encrypted at rest

All conversation content is stored encrypted. Your health conversations stay private.

✓
Anonymous feedback collection

When you choose between A/B responses, that feedback is not linked to your account or identity.

✓
No data selling, ever

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for any purpose beyond operating this service.

✓
Right to delete

You can clear your conversation history at any time. Contact the administrator to delete your account entirely.

✓
HttpOnly cookies

Auth tokens are stored in HttpOnly cookies — inaccessible to JavaScript, protecting against XSS attacks.

🛡️

Built for healthcare privacy

MIRA is designed with the sensitivity of medical data in mind. From JWT-signed sessions to bcrypt password hashing and encrypted message storage, every layer of the stack prioritises your privacy. This is not an afterthought — it's foundational.
